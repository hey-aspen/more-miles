More Miles is designed to make your running data useful without turning it into a product.

Last updated 13 August 2026

Overview

More Miles does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your health data. The app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

What More Miles reads

If you choose to connect Apple Health, More Miles may read the categories you approve: running and walking workouts, workout-linked heart rate and running dynamics, resting heart rate, and date of birth. It also stores goals, gear assignments, preferences, and summaries generated from your activity.

How it is used

Information is processed on your device to organize activities, calculate progress and relative effort, estimate training zones, and create images you choose to share.

Storage and sharing

Imported More Miles data is stored locally on your device. More Miles does not sell health information or upload it to an app-operated server. Content leaves the app only when you explicitly copy, save, or share it through Apple system features.

Local information remains until you delete it in More Miles Settings or remove the app. Deleting More Miles data does not change original Apple Health workouts.

Your choices

Apple Health access is optional. You can review or revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → More Miles.
