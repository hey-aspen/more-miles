Contact

Email feedback@run-trace.com. Including your iOS version, recording app or device, and a screenshot helps us investigate quickly.

An activity is missing

Open More Miles Settings, make sure Apple Health is connected, then sync. You can review permissions in Health → Sharing → Apps → More Miles.

Some metrics are unavailable

More Miles shows data associated with the activity in Apple Health. What is available depends on the recording device, granted permissions, and the samples saved by that activity. More Miles never fabricates missing workout metrics.

Delete local data

Open More Miles Settings and choose Delete App Data. This removes local More Miles records while leaving original Apple Health workouts untouched.

Privacy
